## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: PDF and print keep hollow booking posture

Printed and downloaded guidebook documents MUST NOT invent live prices, availability, or reservation claims. If a booking area appears in the document, it MUST remain empty or explicitly unavailable—consistent with the on-screen hollow booking placeholder.

#### Scenario: No rates in exported document

- **WHEN** a guest prints or downloads a guidebook PDF for a draft trip
- **THEN** the document shows no fake hotel, flight, or activity rates and does not claim a reservation exists
